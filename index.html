Signatures

Seven plates our guests come back for, and the parts of Andhra and Telangana they come from.

  • Hyderabad, Telangana

    Chicken Dum Biryani

    Long-grain basmati layered over marinated chicken, sealed and slow-cooked on dum. Served with mirchi ka salan and raita, the way Hyderabad eats it.

  • Konaseema, Godavari delta

    Konaseema Kodi Chips

    Thin strips of chicken fried crisp and tossed with curry leaves, green chillies and roasted cashews. A favourite from the coconut country of the Godavari delta.

  • Rayalaseema, Andhra Pradesh

    Ragi Sangati with Naatukodi Pulusu

    Soft balls of finger millet and rice with a fiery country-chicken curry. Break the sangati with your fingers and dip it in the pulusu.

  • Karimnagar, Telangana

    Karimnagar Fried Wings

    Chicken wings in a Telangana red-chilli marinade, fried until the edges crackle.

  • Telangana

    Golinchina Mushroom

    Golinchina is how Telangana pan-roasts meat with onion, chilli and garam masala. We cook mushrooms the same way, for vegetarians who like heat.

  • Kokapet, Hyderabad

    Paneer Kurkure

    Paneer in a crunchy, spiced coating, fried golden outside and soft within. A favourite with our vegetarian guests.

  • Hyderabad, Telangana

    Apricot Delight

    Stewed apricot under a layer of silky cream, topped with chopped nuts and served chilled. The sweet way to finish a Telugu meal, in a small cup or a large one to share.

Our story

Every Telugu family has a dish that means home. A grandmother's natukodi pulusu, a festival pulao, pappu with a spoon of ghee and avakaya. The Spice Kitchen cooks those recipes from both Telugu states, and serves them the way a Telugu host would.
