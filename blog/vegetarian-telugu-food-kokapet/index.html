Vegetarian Telugu food in Kokapet: what to order at TSK

13 November 2026 · 9 min read

Golinchina Mushroom, one of our vegetarian starter choices.

For vegetarian Telugu food in Kokapet, start with the meal you want: Tomato Pappu with rice, Gutti Vankaya Kura with an accompaniment, or our vegetarian bagara rice combination. Add Chitti Garelu for a urad dal starter or Golinchina Mushroom for mushrooms with onion, green chilli and spices.

We serve these dishes at TSK The Spice Kitchen on Gandipet Main Road, Hyderabad. Ours is a mixed kitchen serving vegetarian, egg and non-vegetarian food. Use this guide to compare vegetarian choices, work out a sample order and check what to ask before booking.

Six vegetarian Telugu dishes, compared

Chitti Garelu and Golinchina Mushroom start the meal; pappu and curries give you a main course. The bagara rice combination already names its accompaniments. The comparison below separates those roles so you can choose what you need.

Three ways to put your vegetarian meal together

Choose your main course first, then decide whether you want a starter and which accompaniment suits your meal.

Dal and rice

Pair Tomato Pappu with Steamed Rice. Add plain Chitti Garelu if you would like a urad dal starter alongside the dal-and-rice meal.

A rice combination with a mushroom starter

Choose Bagara Rice with Tomato Fry & Gutti Vankaya, then add Golinchina Mushroom if you want a separate starter. The named combo brings together the rice and its listed accompaniments.

A curry with breads

Choose Kaju Tomato Curry with Pulka or a bread from our Indian breads section. Tell us how many people are eating and what else you plan to share so we can discuss quantities.

These are meal-planning suggestions rather than fixed packages or serving guarantees. Ask us about availability and quantities for your particular order.

Would you prefer biryani, pulao or a curry with breads?

Our vegetarian biryani choices include Veg Biryani, Paneer Biryani and Mushroom Biryani. For pulao, consider Vankaya Vepudu Pulao or Pachi Mirchi Kaju Paneer Pulao.

Before You Order Vegetarian Food at TSK

Answers about our menu, dietary preferences, table bookings and catering.

Is TSK a pure vegetarian restaurant?

No. We serve vegetarian, egg and non-vegetarian dishes in a mixed kitchen. Please discuss any ingredient or preparation requirements with our team before ordering.

Which vegetarian Telugu dishes are available at TSK?

Our menu lists Chitti Garelu, Golinchina Mushroom, Gutti Vankaya Kura, Tomato Pappu and Kaju Tomato Curry. It also includes Bagara Rice with Tomato Fry & Gutti Vankaya. Confirm current availability when you order.

Do you serve vegetarian biryani and pulao?

Yes. We list vegetarian biryanis with vegetables, paneer or mushrooms, alongside vegetarian pulaos. Browse the biryani and pulao sections to choose your dish.

Can I ask for less spicy vegetarian food?

Yes. Tell us your preference before ordering so we can discuss suitable dishes and the adjustments available. We will confirm what is possible for your chosen preparation.

Can you prepare food without onion or garlic?

Please ask before placing your order. Availability depends on the dish and preparation. Our menu does not establish a separate no-onion, no-garlic menu.

Are the vegetarian dishes vegan or suitable for food allergies?

A vegetarian label does not confirm that a dish is vegan or suitable for a particular allergy. Tell us which ingredients you need to avoid so we can discuss your order. We do not promise allergen-free preparation.

How do I book for 10 or more guests?

Call +91 99490 51777 to discuss your date, preferred time, guest count and seating needs instead of using the online table-request form.

Can I enquire about vegetarian catering?

Yes. We take catering enquiries for groups of 8 or more people across Hyderabad. Share your date, location, guest count and vegetarian preferences. Our team will confirm the available dishes, quantities and quote. Read our catering information to plan your enquiry.
