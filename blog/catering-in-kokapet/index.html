Catering Across Hyderabad: Plan Your Event Menu with TSK

7 November 2026

A selection of food from our restaurant menu. Photo illustrates our dishes, not a fixed catering package.

With a 4.6+ Google rating, we focus on flavour, quality food, generous quantities and attentive customer service. Menu choices and charges depend on your order quantity, while delivery, buffet setup, serving equipment and staff can be discussed for each event.

Order at least one day ahead; three days’ notice is preferred. Share your date, location and guest count for a personalised quote.

What can you order for catering?

Our catering choices include biryanis, pulaos, kebabs and Telugu specials from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. We also take bulk biryani orders. Both vegetarian and non-vegetarian choices are available; we’ll confirm the dishes for your date and quantity when preparing your quote.

Start with our current restaurant menu to share the flavours you enjoy. It is a useful starting point for a conversation, rather than a fixed catering package or catering price list. If a particular dish is essential to your event, mention it before finalising the order.

For a closer look at one of our Telugu dishes, read our guide to ragi sangati with natukodi pulusu. If you would like it for your gathering, ask us to confirm its availability and suitability for your order.

Build the meal around your guests

A good place to start is the main course. Would your guests enjoy biryani, pulao, or a Telugu meal with curries? Once you have that starting point, discuss starters and accompaniments with us. A shorter selection that suits your group can make planning easier than choosing several dishes before considering quantities.

For an office meal, share the serving time, vegetarian and non-vegetarian guest counts, and any delivery or building-access instructions. For a house party or birthday, tell us whether food will be the main meal or part of a longer gathering. These details help us discuss quantities around the way you plan to serve.

For a pooja or vegetarian gathering, make your food and preparation requirements clear at the start. We operate a mixed kitchen serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian food, so please discuss any specific restrictions with our team before confirming.

Tell us whether your group prefers mild, medium or spicy food. We’ll discuss what is possible for your chosen dishes. If children and adults have different preferences, mention that too, rather than assuming every dish can be adjusted in the same way.

How much notice do we need?

We accept catering orders one day before the event, but three days in advance is preferred. The extra time lets us discuss your menu, quantity and arrangements before the event day.

When you contact us, share both the event date and the time you want to serve the food. For a next-day order, send those details along with your guest count and preferred dishes so we can confirm the arrangements. Your enquiry becomes a confirmed order only after you and our team have agreed the details.

How are catering prices and quantities decided?

Our catering menu and charges depend on the quantity ordered. We’ll prepare a quote around the food and arrangements discussed for your event; there is no fixed package or per-person price listed in this guide.

Along with the total guest count, tell us how many people want vegetarian or non-vegetarian food and whether you are serving other food at the event. For a bulk biryani order, ask us to recommend the quantity for your group rather than estimating portions from a restaurant menu size label.

Before confirming, review the dish names, quantities, total price, serving or delivery time and any agreed services with us. If your plans change, contact us to discuss the revised order and quote.

Delivery, buffet setup, equipment and staff

Delivery, setup, serving equipment and serving staff can be discussed for each order. They are not automatically included with every catering enquiry. Tell us whether you need food delivered, help arranging the serving area, equipment or people to serve your guests.

We discuss catering requests across Hyderabad from our Kokapet restaurant. Share the exact event location so we can confirm delivery availability and any location-related charges. If your venue has a delivery slot, lift restrictions or a designated setup area, include those details when asking for a quote.

Ask us for a catering quote

Send us these details on WhatsApp to get the conversation started:

  • Event date and preferred serving time.
  • Number of guests and occasion.
  • Delivery area or venue location.
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian preferences, including spice preferences.
  • Preferred dishes or the kind of meal you have in mind.
  • Any delivery, setup, equipment or serving-staff requirements.

You don’t need to arrive with a finished menu. Share what you know, and we’ll discuss the choices and quantities with you. Keep the final agreed menu and quote handy when checking your event arrangements.

Before You Book Catering with Us

Quick answers to help you plan your gathering and request a useful quote.

What is the minimum number of people for TSK catering?

Our catering minimum is eight people. Share your guest count, event date and food preferences so we can discuss your order.

Can I order catering one day before my event?

Yes. We accept orders one day in advance, though we prefer three days’ notice. Contact us with your date, serving time and quantity to confirm the arrangements.

Do you have fixed catering packages or a per-person price?

Menu choices and charges depend on the quantity ordered. Message us with your guest count and preferred dishes for a quote; this guide does not list a fixed package or per-person price.

Do you take bulk biryani and office meal orders?

Yes. We take bulk biryani orders and catering enquiries for office meals. Tell us the guest count, preferred food, location and serving time so we can discuss quantities and a quote.

Can we choose vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes?

Yes. Both are available. Tell us how many guests want each and discuss your spice preferences. We operate a mixed kitchen, so explain any specific preparation requirements before confirming.

Are delivery, buffet setup, equipment and serving staff included?

These can be discussed per order and are not automatically included. Tell us what your venue needs, and confirm the agreed services and charges in your quote.

Do you deliver catering outside Kokapet?

We discuss catering requests across Hyderabad. Send your exact event location so we can confirm delivery availability, timing and any related charges for your order.

How do I confirm a catering order?

Start with a WhatsApp enquiry or call. Review the menu, quantities, total price, timing and any agreed services with our team. An enquiry alone does not confirm an order; we’ll agree the details with you.

Why choose TSK for catering in Hyderabad?

We focus on quality food, generous portions and attentive customer service for gatherings of eight to 200+ guests. Our Kokapet team discusses your menu, guest count and event requirements with you before confirming the order.

Can your team help us choose the right menu and quantities?

Yes. Tell us your guest count, vegetarian and non-vegetarian preferences, spice preferences and how you plan to serve the meal. We’ll help you discuss suitable dishes and quantities, then agree the menu and quote with you.
